What sound is your vehicle making?

Select the acoustic symptom heard when turning the key or driving:

Rapid Clicking / Buzzing
Starter solenoid chatters but engine does not crank over.
Loud Metal Grinding
Harsh scraping noise from wheels, transmission, or brakes.
High-Pitched Squeal
Piercing belt whine from alternator or water pump.
Heavy Engine Knock
Deep rhythmic metallic thumping inside engine block.
Complete Silence
Turning ignition produces zero noise; instrument cluster dark.
No Abnormal Noise
Engine sound is normal or issue is external (e.g. flat tyre).

Do you see smoke, steam, or detect odors?

Visual cues provide crucial thermal and fluid leak signals:

Critical
White Steam from Bonnet
Pressurized steam billows from radiator or expansion tank.
Urgent
Visible Flat Tyre / Rim Drop
Tyre rubber is collapsed against tarmac or rim is touching.
Warning
Sweet Syrup Aroma / Green Puddle
Ethylene glycol coolant leaking beneath radiator.
Warning
Dark Engine Oil Puddle
Slick viscous black puddle forming under oil pan.
Hazard
Burning Rubber / Electrical Odor
Acrid wire insulation smell or slipping belt friction.
Normal
No Smoke or Fluid Leaks
Clean dry pavement; no burning smell detected.

Are any dashboard warning lights illuminated?

Instrument cluster lamps display direct OBD-II ECU telemetry:

🌡 High Temperature Lamp (Red)
Coolant temp gauge pegged in RED zone; thermal alert.
⚡ Battery / Alternator Symbol
Charging system voltage below 11.8V threshold.
🛞 TPMS Low Tyre Pressure
Tyre pressure sensor triggered below safety threshold.
🩸 Oil Pressure Can Symbol (Red)
Critical low oil pressure; risk of immediate engine seizure.
⚙ Check Engine (Amber MIL)
Emissions, misfire, or transmission control fault.
✔ No Warning Lights Active
Dashboard lamps extinguished normally.

What is the vehicle's current mobility status?

Can the vehicle roll, crawl, or is it immobilized on the road?

Completely Stranded / Immobile
Engine dead; vehicle cannot move under its own power.
Tyre Deflated; Rolling Risks Rim Damage
Engine runs, but rolling further will destroy alloy wheel.
Severe Shuddering / Hazardous to Merge
Misfires or transmission slippage makes highway speed unsafe.
Parked Safely on Shoulder / Off-Road
Vehicle safely positioned clear of active traffic lanes.
